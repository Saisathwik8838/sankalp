Component sheet

Design decisions where the brief was open

  1. Day states use shape + icon + color: completed = filled check tile; missed = dashed border with dash; today = round outline; upcoming = faded; final day = arch-topped “diya” tile with a flame.
  2. Missed days are “clay”, never red, and copy says “A day was missed”, never “You failed”. Streak card shows current and best without a broken-streak alert.
  3. Dark theme lifts saffron to #F0793A so it stays ≥ 4.5:1 on #1A110D.
  4. Counter screen has almost no chrome: one big ring, one number, small toggles. Bead ring is a variant of the same control.
  5. The complete button lists what remains (“1 practice left”) instead of just going gray.
  6. Completion is quiet: a slow glow behind a lit diya, disabled under reduced motion; no confetti.
  7. Header image slot is an empty dashed frame the user can fill; no deity imagery is drawn anywhere.
  8. Scripture text is placeholder only. Reader, Insights, Onboarding and Settings are shown as compact key frames rather than every step.
